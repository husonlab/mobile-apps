# <img src="assets/icons/phylosketch.png" width="64" alt="" style="vertical-align: middle"> PhyloSketch App

PhyloSketch App is for drawing rooted phylogenetic trees and networks. Draw nodes and edges with a finger or
pencil, label them, and the app keeps track of what kind of network has been drawn (tree, tree-child,
tree-based, and so on) and reports its properties. A photo of a printed tree can be captured and turned into
an editable network, including the recognition of its labels. Networks can be imported from and exported to
Newick format and laid out automatically.

**Paper:** Daniel H. Huson. Sketch, capture and layout phylogenies. *PLOS Computational Biology* 21(12): e1013805
(2025). [doi:10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013805](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013805)

**Desktop program:** [github.com/husonlab/phylosketch2](https://github.com/husonlab/phylosketch2) (GPL 3).

## Working with files

Documents are kept in the app's own folder on the device. The files tab of the app lists them, and the same
folder is visible in the Files app under *On My iPhone* (or *On My iPad*), where files can be copied, renamed or
moved to iCloud Drive. To bring a file into the app, share it from another app, from Mail or from Files and
choose the app, or copy its text and use *Import > Paste into new file* in the files tab. To get data out, use
*Export* in the files tab or the export items in the document itself: they open the system share sheet, from
which files and images can be saved, sent or copied.
