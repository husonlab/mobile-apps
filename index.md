# Phylogenetics on iPhone and iPad

These apps are native iOS versions of open-source desktop programs for phylogenetics and biochemical network analysis
developed by Daniel H. Huson and colleagues at the University of Tübingen. They are built with Java and JavaFX,
compiled to native code, and share the full computational core with their desktop counterparts, so the results
and file formats are identical on both.

The apps run entirely on the device. They do not collect data, do not need an account and do not connect to the
internet. See the [privacy policy](privacy) and the [support page](support).

Android versions are planned.

## <img src="assets/icons/splitstree.png" width="48" alt="" style="vertical-align: middle"> SplitsTree App

Compute and visualize unrooted and rooted phylogenetic trees and networks from distances, aligned sequences,
trees or splits. The app provides the algorithms of the SplitsTree desktop program, including neighbor-net,
split decomposition, consensus networks and the phylogenetic outline, and the same interactive viewers.

[Details](splitstree) ·
[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-024-02406-3) (Huson and Bryant, Nature Methods 2024) ·
[Desktop program](https://github.com/husonlab/splitstree6) ·
App Store: coming soon

## <img src="assets/icons/phylosketch.png" width="48" alt="" style="vertical-align: middle"> PhyloSketch App

Draw rooted phylogenetic trees and networks with a finger or pencil, capture them from a photo of a printed
figure, lay them out and export them in Newick format. The app reports the mathematical properties of the
network that is being drawn.

[Details](phylosketch) ·
[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.04.01.646633) (Huson, Sketch, capture and layout phylogenies, 2025) ·
[Desktop program](https://github.com/husonlab/phylosketch2) ·
App Store: coming soon

## <img src="assets/icons/catrenet.png" width="48" alt="" style="vertical-align: middle"> CatReNet App

Analyze (auto-)catalytic reaction networks: given a set of catalyzed reactions and a food set, compute maximal
RAFs, CAFs and pseudo-RAFs and other autocatalytic subsystems, and visualize and animate the networks.

[Details](catrenet) ·
[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btae515) (Huson, Xavier and Steel, Bioinformatics 2024) ·
[Desktop program](https://github.com/husonlab/catrenet) ·
App Store: coming soon

## <img src="assets/icons/phyloparallelograms.png" width="48" alt="" style="vertical-align: middle"> PhyloParallelograms App

Compare multiple rooted phylogenetic trees on the same taxa using phylogenetic parallelograms, a visualization in
which the trees are drawn in parallel in one coordinate space, so that shared structure and conflicts are
immediately visible.

[Details](phyloparallelograms) ·
Paper: Huson, Cetinkaya and Zhang, manuscript in preparation ·
[Desktop program](https://github.com/husonlab/phyloparallelograms) ·
App Store: coming soon
