# Support

## Getting help

- **Questions and bug reports:** open an issue in the
  [mobile-apps issue tracker](https://github.com/husonlab/mobile-apps/issues), or send an email to
  [daniel.huson@uni-tuebingen.de](mailto:daniel.huson@uni-tuebingen.de). Please name the app and the iOS version
  and describe what you did and what happened. A screenshot or an example file helps.
- **Documentation:** the apps share their methods and file formats with the desktop programs, whose manuals
  and example data are linked from each app's page: [SplitsTree](splitstree), [PhyloSketch](phylosketch),
  [CatReNet](catrenet), [PhyloParallelograms](phyloparallelograms).
- **Log:** each app has a log pane in the files tab (the *Log* panel at the bottom). Its contents can be copied
  and pasted into a bug report.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where are my files?** In the app's own folder, listed in the files tab and visible in the Files app under
*On My iPhone* or *On My iPad*. The apps save documents automatically shortly after each change.

**How do I open a file from Mail, Files or another app?** Use the share sheet and choose the app, or copy the
file's text and use *Import > Paste into new file* in the files tab.

**How do I get an image or a result out?** Use *Export* in the files tab or the export items of the document.
They open the system share sheet, from which you can save to Files or Photos, send by Mail or AirDrop, or copy.

**Can I use the same files on my computer?** Yes. The apps read and write the same formats as the desktop
programs, so a document can be moved back and forth through iCloud Drive or the Files app.

**Is there an Android version?** Not yet; Android versions are planned.
