# <img src="assets/icons/phyloparallelograms.png" width="64" alt="" style="vertical-align: middle"> PhyloParallelograms App

PhyloParallelograms App compares several rooted phylogenetic trees on the same taxa. The trees are drawn in
parallel in one coordinate space as a phylogenetic parallelogram, which highlights the evolutionary structure
that the trees share and makes conflicting signals immediately visible. Trees are read in Newick or Nexus
format, can be rerooted and filtered, and the resulting figure can be exported as an image or the consensus
network as Newick.

**Paper:** Daniel H. Huson, Banu Cetinkaya and Louxin Zhang. Phylogenetic parallelograms for visual comparison of
discordant trees. Manuscript under review.
[doi:10.64898/2026.09.18.752558](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.18.752558)

**Desktop program:** [github.com/husonlab/phyloparallelograms](https://github.com/husonlab/phyloparallelograms)
(GPL 3).

## Working with files

Documents are kept in the app's own folder on the device. The files tab of the app lists them, and the same
folder is visible in the Files app under *On My iPhone* (or *On My iPad*), where files can be copied, renamed or
moved to iCloud Drive. To bring a file into the app, share it from another app, from Mail or from Files and
choose the app, or copy its text and use *Import > Paste into new file* in the files tab. To get data out, use
*Export* in the files tab or the export items in the document itself: they open the system share sheet, from
which files and images can be saved, sent or copied.
