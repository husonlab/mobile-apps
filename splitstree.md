# <img src="assets/icons/splitstree.png" width="64" alt="" style="vertical-align: middle"> SplitsTree App

SplitsTree App computes and visualizes phylogenetic trees and networks. It reads distances, aligned
DNA, RNA or protein sequences, trees and splits in Nexus, FASTA, Newick, PHYLIP and other common formats,
and offers the methods of the [SplitsTree desktop program](https://github.com/husonlab/splitstree6): distance
methods such as neighbor-joining, neighbor-net and split decomposition, consensus trees and networks, the
phylogenetic outline, rooted networks, and interactive tree, network and tanglegram viewers. Analyses are set up
in the same workflow as on the desktop, and documents can be exchanged with the desktop program without
conversion.

The app is designed for the iPad and also runs on the iPhone.

**Paper:** Daniel H. Huson and David Bryant. The SplitsTree App: interactive analysis and visualization using
phylogenetic trees and networks. *Nature Methods* (2024).
[doi:10.1038/s41592-024-02406-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-024-02406-3)

**Desktop program:** [github.com/husonlab/splitstree6](https://github.com/husonlab/splitstree6) (GPL 3).

## Working with files

Documents are kept in the app's own folder on the device. The files tab of the app lists them, and the same
folder is visible in the Files app under *On My iPhone* (or *On My iPad*), where files can be copied, renamed or
moved to iCloud Drive. To bring a file into the app, share it from another app, from Mail or from Files and
choose the app, or copy its text and use *Import > Paste into new file* in the files tab. To get data out, use
*Export* in the files tab or the export items in the document itself: they open the system share sheet, from
which files and images can be saved, sent or copied.
