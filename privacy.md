# Privacy policy

*Effective 9 November 2026. Applies to the SplitsTree, PhyloSketch, CatReNet and PhyloParallelograms apps for
iOS and iPadOS.*

## Summary

The apps do not collect, store or transmit any personal data. They work entirely on your device, need no account
and make no network connections.

## What the apps do with your data

**Your documents.** The files you create or import are stored in the app's own folder on your device. They
leave the device only when you share or export them yourself, through the iOS share sheet, the Files app or
iCloud Drive, and then only to the destination you choose. The apps have no server and never upload anything.

**Photos.** PhyloSketch App can import a picture through the system photo picker. The picker runs outside the
app and hands over only the single picture you select; the app has no access to your photo library.

**Clipboard.** When you choose to paste, the apps read the clipboard to import text; they write to it only when
you copy something.

**Settings.** Preferences such as the dark-mode setting and recently used options are kept in the app's private
storage on the device.

## What the apps do not do

The apps contain no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting libraries of their own and no accounts
or sign-in. They do not access your location, contacts, microphone or camera, and they do not connect to the
internet. If you have enabled sharing of analytics and crash data with app developers in your iOS settings,
Apple may provide anonymous crash reports to us under Apple's own privacy terms; we receive no personal data
from these.

## App Store

Purchases and downloads are handled by Apple. We do not receive your name, address or payment details. Apple's
privacy policy applies to those transactions.

## Children

The apps are scientific tools and are not directed at children. As they collect no data, no special provisions
apply.

## Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.

## Your rights

Because the apps process no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a
question about privacy, write to the address above.
